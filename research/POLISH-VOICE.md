# The Polish Voice -- verified quotes, proverbs and vocabulary

Everything here was verified against a named printed source, a PAN dictionary
entry (WSJP), or pl.wikiquote raw wikitext. Anything unverified was cut. Use
freely. Do not add to this list from memory.

ASCII transliteration in body; correct Polish spelling noted where it matters.

---

## THE SINGLE BEST LINE IN POLISH ON DRINKING

"Poniewierac ma, a nie smakowac."
  -- Janusz, in Wojciech Smarzowski's WESELE (2004). Wikiquote's note: "o wodce."
  "It's meant to knock you about, not to taste good."

Five words containing an entire philosophy. If the book uses one quote, this
is it. Note how completely it condemns Bober's PBR, which is a beer you drink
BECAUSE it does not knock you about.

## JERZY PILCH, "POD MOCNYM ANIOLEM" (1999 novel, Nike Prize)

The deepest literary seam available. NOTE: the wikiquote page is the NOVEL,
not Smarzowski's 2014 film. Cite the novel.

The catalogue of reasons -- the book's rival answer to its own title question:
  "Pije, bo pije. Pije, bo lubie. Pije, bo sie boje. [...] Pije, kiedy jestem
  szczesliwie zakochany. Pije, bo daremnie szukam milosci. [...] Pije, kiedy
  wypijam pierwszy kieliszek, i pije, kiedy wypijam ostatni kieliszek, wtedy
  pije tym bardziej, poniewaz ostatniego kieliszka nie wypilem nigdy."
  "I drink because I drink. I drink because I like it. I drink because I am
  afraid. [...] I drink when I am happily in love. I drink because I search for
  love in vain. [...] I drink when I drink the first glass, and I drink when I
  drink the last glass -- and then I drink all the more, because I have never
  once drunk the last glass."

The national catalogue -- the most important paragraph in the dossier:
  "Pili, bo Polska byla pod moskiewskim jarzmem, i pili w euforii po
  wyzwoleniu. Pili, bo Polak zostal papiezem [...] Pili, gdy byli sami, i pili,
  gdy ktokolwiek pojawil sie obok nich. Pili, kiedy Polska zwyciezala, i pili,
  kiedy Polska przegrywala."
  "They drank because Poland was under the Moscow yoke, and they drank in the
  euphoria after liberation. They drank because a Pole became Pope [...] They
  drank when they were alone and they drank when anyone at all appeared beside
  them. They drank when Poland won and they drank when Poland lost."

  "Gdybys nie pil, bylbys szczesliwszy, ale rozumialbys malo."
  "If you didn't drink you'd be happier, but you'd understand little."

  "Pijakowi wstyd pic, ale pijakowi jeszcze gorszy wstyd: nie pic."
  "It shames a drunk to drink, but it shames a drunk worse not to drink."

  "Nie ma zadnej filozofii picia, jest jedynie technika picia."
  "There is no philosophy of drinking, only a technique of drinking."

The washing machines -- for a chapter on what drinking costs an artist:
  "Przepilem pieniadze na kupno nowej pralki [...] przepilem wszystkie pralki
  swiata. Jaka dusze ma czlowiek, ktory wie, ze przepil wszystkie pralki
  swiata? Odpowiadam: dusze ma uskrzydlona, a mysl jego wiruje jak beben w
  ostatniej fazie odsaczania."
  "I drank away the money for a new washing machine [...] I drank away every
  washing machine in the world. What kind of soul does a man have who knows he
  has drunk away every washing machine in the world? I answer: a winged soul,
  and his thought spins like a drum in the final phase of the spin cycle."
  (The verb "przepic" -- to drink away money -- is one Polish word.)

## OTHER LITERARY VOICES (all sourced)

Marek Hlasko, "Umarli sa wsrod nas" (Dziela zebrane, Da Capo 1989, s. 96):
  "Niech nie osmiela sie wydawac sadow o wodce ci, ktorym nie jest ona
  potrzebna. Jesli ludzkosc osiagnela dotychczas cokolwiek trwalego w sensie
  ducha, to wlasnie alkohol."
  "Let those who have no need of vodka not dare pass judgement on it. If
  humanity has achieved anything lasting in the realm of the spirit, it is
  alcohol."

Film "Petla" (scr. Has / Hlasko):
  "Wodka jest prawda, ktora rozumie sie zawsze zbyt pozno."
  "Vodka is a truth one always understands too late."

Edward Stachura, "Cala jaskrawosc":
  "Wodka to jest czlowiek, z ktorym mozna porozmawiac."
  "Vodka is a person you can talk to."

Wladyslaw Reymont, "Chlopi" t.1 (Gebethner i Wolff, 1925, s. 306):
  "Chrzest przyjmuj woda, wesele polewaj wodka, a pogrzeb -- plakaniem."
  "Take a christening with water, drench a wedding with vodka, and a funeral
  with weeping." -- Excellent chapter epigraph.

Kazik Staszewski, "Cztery pokoje" (song):
  "I wlasciwie kto wodki nie pije ten jest wywrotowcem [...] Swiadomie
  uszczuplajacym dochody panstwa -- bezideowcem"
  "And really, whoever doesn't drink vodka is a subversive -- knowingly
  depleting state revenue, a man without ideals."
  -- The sharpest line available on the STATE's stake. Pair with chapter 3.

Kacper Poblocki, "Chamstwo" (Czarne, 2021, s. 260):
  "Trzysta lat panszczyzny to rowniez trzysta lat konsekwentnego rozpijania
  polskiego ludu. Wodka stala sie wrecz najbardziej dochodowa galezia panskiej
  gospodarki -- 'prawdziwa mennica', jak to ujal ksiaze Czartoryski."
  "Three hundred years of serfdom were also three hundred years of the
  consistent getting-drunk of the Polish people. Vodka became the most
  profitable branch of the lordly economy -- 'a real mint,' as Prince
  Czartoryski put it."

Poblocki, "Chamstwo" s. 264 -- the peasant temperance revolt:
  "W latach 1844-1855 setki tysiecy galicyjskich poddanych zlozyly publiczna,
  uroczysta przysiege, ze przestana pic. W niektorych obwodach zdecydowalo sie
  na to nawet trzy czwarte doroslych. [...] Panowie bronili propinacji jako
  swojej 'stalej, integralnej, nieodzownej czesci dochodow' i wnosili o
  zakazanie 'kazan wkraczajacych w stosunek poddanczy'."
  "Between 1844 and 1855 hundreds of thousands of Galician serfs took a public,
  solemn oath to stop drinking. In some districts as many as three quarters of
  adults did so. [...] The lords defended propination as a 'fixed, integral,
  indispensable part of their income' and petitioned for a ban on 'sermons
  trespassing on the relation of serfdom.'"
  -- The peasants organised against the booze; the landlords tried to ban the
  sermons. Chapter 3's best single fact.

## DATED, ATTRIBUTED SAYINGS (16th-18th c.)

  "Albo pij, albo sie bij."           -- Salomon Rysinski, 1618
  "Either drink or fight."
  "Slodko sie pije, gorzko sie placi." -- Stanislaw Jagodynski, 1620
  "Sweetly you drink, bitterly you pay."
  "Pijac zdrowie cudze, tracimy swoje." -- Jozef Zaluski, 1750
  "Drinking another's health, we lose our own."
  "Wiecej zamordowalo pijanstwo aniz miecz." -- Marcin Bialobrzeski, 1581
  "Drunkenness has murdered more than the sword."

Folk syllogism (19th c.), the sourced ancestor of "kto pije, ten zyje":
  "Kto pije, ten spi; kto spi, ten nie grzeszy; kto nie grzeszy, ten bedzie
  zbawiony."
  "He who drinks, sleeps; he who sleeps, does not sin; he who does not sin
  shall be saved."

  "Nie my pierwsi obyczaj picia wprowadzili, pili Grecy, Rzymianie i Sarmaci
  pili."
  "We weren't the first to bring in the custom of drinking; the Greeks drank,
  the Romans drank, and the Sarmatians drank."
  -- A specifically Polish self-justification. Note "Sarmaci."

Adalberg, "Ksiega przyslow" (1889-94), s. 575 -- best-provenanced:
  "Gdziesie upil, tam idz spac."  "Go and sleep it off where you got drunk."

## DICTIONARY-VERIFIED PROVERBS (WSJP PAN)

"Na frasunek dobry trunek" (WSJP 6933) -- THE Polish drinking proverb.
  WSJP definition: "alkohol pomaga zapomniec o problemach i smutkach."
  "For sorrow, good liquor." (Rhymes.)

"Od wodki rozum krotki" (WSJP 7584), full folk couplet as printed in
Dziennik Polski, 10 June 2000:
  "Wszak od wodki rozum krotki, a od piwa glowa sie kiwa."
  "From vodka, short wits; from beer, a nodding head."
  -- The couplet distinguishes vodka from beer. Bober drinks the second half.

## VOCABULARY THAT DOES ARGUMENTATIVE WORK

sklep monopolowy -- still the standard word for an off-licence. A living
  linguistic fossil of the state alcohol monopoly. Worth its own paragraph.

propinacja -- the noble monopoly right to produce and sell alcohol to
  peasants. Granted 1572 (also a 1496 Sejm statute); abolished 1845/1889/1898
  across the three partitions.

zakaska / zagrycha -- the food eaten immediately after a shot. Kosinski (IH
  PAN, 2006): drinking WITHOUT it was rated by a third of Poles surveyed as the
  most "socially harmful" practice. And 15.3% of townsmen used BEER as the
  chaser for vodka.

oblewac -- "to douse/wet," i.e. to celebrate something by drinking on it.
  Kosinski: "No to jak, oblewamy?" was a characteristic PRL phrase.

kac (from German Katzenjammer) -- hangover. WSJP synonyms include kociokwik
  and, gloriously, "tupot bialych mew" -- "the patter of white seagulls."
  Lexicalised forms: "kac gigant," "kac morderca," "kac moralny."

klin -- the hair of the dog. WSJP: "wybijac klin klinem," to drive out a wedge
  with a wedge.

strzemienny -- the stirrup cup, the last round before the party disperses.
  From "strzemie," stirrup: the drink taken with a foot already in the stirrup.
  Diminutive: rozchodniaczek, from "rozchodzic sie," to disperse. Poland has a
  DIMINUTIVE for the drink that is supposed to end the drinking.

pic bruderszaft (WSJP 27583) -- the drink-and-kiss ceremony that moves two
  people from formal "pan/pani" to first names. Filed by PAN under
  "Tradycja -> zwyczaje i obyczaje." Alcohol here literally changes the
  grammar you may use to another person. The single most important entry for
  a book on Polish drinking.

kolega od kieliszka (WSJP 12401) -- "a person with whom close acquaintance
  rests on meetings at which alcohol is drunk." Poland has a dictionary word
  for the friend you only have because you drink together.

pic do lustra (WSJP 24780) -- to drink alone, lit. "to drink to the mirror."
  Polityka, 26 July 2003: "For men, drinking to the mirror is the mark of
  deepest degeneration; for women it is often the beginning."

zalac robaka (WSJP 46283) -- "to drown the worm," to drink to forget.

wchodzic miedzy wodke a zakaske (WSJP 65960) -- to butt in; literally "to get
  between the vodka and the chaser." Polish has an idiom for INTERFERING built
  out of the drinking sequence.

spod budki z piwem (WSJP 24879) -- "from under the beer kiosk," i.e. boorish.
  A whole social class named after a piece of street furniture.

urwal sie film (WSJP 26186) -- blackout. Grammatically a "fraza zdaniowa": the
  FILM is the subject and it breaks ON you, in the dative. You are not the
  agent. Make something of that.

trzezwy jak swinia -- "sober as a pig." A real lexicalised anti-idiom,
  inverting "pijany jak swinia" for comic effect.

na gazie / na bani / na rauszu / na promilach / pod dobra data -- WSJP's
  synonym set for tipsy. "Na promilach" -- "on the per-milles" -- is coined
  from breathalyser units.

bez wodki nie rozbierzesz (WSJP 21194) -- "you can't work it out without
  vodka," used when something is too tangled to understand sober. Nine
  registered variants, several in macaronic mock-Russian ("nie razbieriosz,"
  from Russian razberyosh) -- a Cold War fossil inside a joke idiom.

## CORRECTIONS -- DO NOT PRINT THE FOLLOWING

- "Pijany jak szewc" is NOT in the PAN dictionary. What IS codified is "klnie
  jak szewc" -- in official Polish the cobbler SWEARS, he does not drink. The
  verified drunk-similes are: w trupa, w pestke, w sztok, w drzazgi, jak pijany
  plotu, jak pijane dziecko we mgle.
- "Mis" (Bareja) is not the drinking film its reputation suggests. Its most
  quoted "drinking" line, "No, konicz Wladziu i robimy...", is annotated on
  wikiquote as a CARD GAME. The drift in popular memory is itself a better
  story than the quote.
- "Nie ma mocnych" is a 1974 film title, not a vodka proverb.
- "Alkohol pity z umiarem nie szkodzi nawet w duzych ilosciach" has CONTESTED
  attribution (Himilsbach? Tuwim? Ferdek?). Print as anonymous folk aphorism
  or not at all. It is not from Bareja.
- UNVERIFIED, do not print: "Pij, a rozum miej"; "Gdzie wodka, tam zgoda";
  "Kto pije, ten zyje"; "Wodka rodzi bractwo"; any PRL-era political DRINKING
  joke; the proverb "Polak, Wegier -- dwa bratanki, i do szabli, i do
  szklanki" (only the Kiepscy parody of it is verified).
